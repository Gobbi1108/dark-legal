Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

claudio-dark ("the App") is a private desktop tool used by a single creator to publish his own videos to his own TikTok and YouTube accounts. Contact: canaldarkzinx@gmail.com.

2. Data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via user.info.basic, used only to show which account is connected; permission to upload and publish videos via video.upload and video.publish; and the creator info returned before each post (privacy options, interaction settings, max video duration).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and set their title, description and schedule on the connected channel, through the YouTube API Services.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own accounts. We do not sell, rent, share or analyze user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not access data from other users.

4. Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a local file that is never uploaded to any server. No data is stored on servers operated by us.

5. Third-party services

The App uses the TikTok API and the YouTube API Services. Their own policies apply: TikTok Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) or at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; you can also ask us to delete them by writing to the contact email.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
